JCSS Library#

Reports, papers, and original documents from the Joint Committee on Structural Safety. Reading editions retain the source wording and references in the Probabilistic Model Code layout. Presentations and other event documents are available in their original formats.

Reports and guidance#

Browse reports and guidance by subject, including risk assessment, risk acceptance and LQI, and governance and climate. Each report has a reading edition and an original PDF.

Special issues and papers#

Browse special issues and selected papers associated with JCSS. References link to journal issues, DOI records, and institutional repositories. Full-text availability is determined by the linked provider.

Workshop archive#

Browse the workshop archive by date and place. Each entry brings together the available programme, presentations, papers and conclusions.

Education#

Browse education material by course or webinar series. Related announcements, registration forms, posters, and supplementary reading share an entry.

Notices#

Browse publication notices and press releases. Publication notices provide information about the referenced work.

About the Library#

The Library collects publications and material from JCSS courses, workshops, and meetings. It includes reports that can be read online and original documents available for download. The Probabilistic Model Code is available separately. Visit the JCSS website for information about the committee and its activities.

Original documents#

Online editions retain the text, equations, figures, and references of the original documents. Select Original PDF from the download menu to view the original layout and page numbers. Presentations are available as downloads from their catalogue entries. Dates and draft labels refer to the original documents, including drafts retained in the collection.

Citing the material#

Cite the individual work using its authors, title, publication date, and edition. The Source and citation section of each online edition provides these details. Use the original PDF when referring to page numbers. For example:

Joint Committee on Structural Safety (JCSS). (2025). Protocol for Risk-Informed Governance and Communication. April 2025.

For journal articles, use the published citation and DOI. Copyright and reuse conditions remain those of the original work.

If you find an error or a broken link, report it on GitHub. Please include the document title and the affected page or section.
