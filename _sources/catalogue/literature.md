## The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future <a class="library-permalink" href="#literature-2025-jcss-anniversary" aria-label="Permanent link to The Joint Committee of Structural Safety: past, present and a perspective on the future">¶</a>

(2025-03). *Structural Safety*, 113.

Editors: Maria Pina Limongelli, Michael Havbro Faber, Jochen Köhler, and Ton Vrouwenvelder.

This anniversary issue marks fifty years of JCSS work on structural safety and considers its future development. Contributions examine the committee’s history, the development of structural reliability methods, and proposed advances to the Probabilistic Model Code. Together, they connect the organisation’s work with the methods and probabilistic models used in structural design and assessment.

## Structural Safety 19(3): JCSS papers <a class="library-permalink" href="#literature-1997-jcss-papers" aria-label="Permanent link to Structural Safety 19(3): JCSS papers">¶</a>

(1997). *Structural Safety*, 19(3).

Devoted to the work of JCSS, this issue brings together an early account of the Probabilistic Model Code and studies of the methods supporting probabilistic design. Its papers address reliability and decision analysis, structural dynamics, and uncertainty in numerical analysis, including stochastic finite elements. The collection provides background to the development of the PMC and the treatment of uncertainty in structural engineering calculations.
